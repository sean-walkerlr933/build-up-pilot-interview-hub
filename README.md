## What Is Build Up Pilot?

Build Up Pilot gives students and early-career learners a central place to manage placement preparation and job-search work. Through its web interface, users can assess their abilities, strengthen their resumes, rehearse interview scenarios, and find career-related guidance.

The platform combines AI-supported preparation with practical employment tasks, including applying for jobs and booking time with mentors. It is built for students and freshers who are developing confidence, presenting their experience more effectively, and getting ready to pursue work opportunities.

## Using the Platform

A common preparation path looks like this:

1. Launch Build Up Pilot in your browser.
2. Complete a skill assessment to understand your current preparation priorities.
3. Run a resume analysis and make improvements to your application materials.
4. Work through mock interview practice.
5. Explore job applications and keep track of the next actions.
6. Schedule a mentor session if you need further guidance.

The exact sequence and available options can differ between deployed builds and according to which platform features are enabled.
